The mark

Two identical corner brackets in 180° rotational symmetry on a 32-unit grid: stroke 6, inner channel 4. Every rule below is a consequence of that drawing, which is why the design has no border radius anywhere and frames content with corners rather than boxes.

Brackets and ticks

The core primitive. Two corners imply a frame without drawing one — a full border would be a card, and a card is the generic shape this design exists to avoid. A single corner marks state: the active nav item, the hovered row, the role a list belongs to.

Badges

Awarded, not decorative. Rendered at 16px in a list row, 22px on a profile, 25px in prose. Each one carries a tooltip with its name, so the icon never has to be self-explanatory.

List rows

72px, virtualised, and capped at 512px of viewport. Hover grows a corner tick in the role's colour rather than lifting or shadowing the row — one pseudo-element, same vocabulary as everything else.

Overlay surfaces

Dropdowns, tooltips and selects sit on content1, one step above the canvas. These are heroui components, and their semantic tokens have to be set explicitly — unset, they fall back to stock greys that belong to no palette.

Motion

Four durations and two curves, all of them short. Motion here confirms an action or assembles the mark; it never decorates. Everything below collapses to a fade under prefers-reduced-motion.

--duration-press 140ms — scale(0.97) on :active
--duration-hover 160ms — colour and background
--duration-bracket 220ms — the corners converging, +40ms on the second
--ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)
