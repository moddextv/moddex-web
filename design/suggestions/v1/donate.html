moddex runs on one server and no advertising.

The index is 2.7 million accounts and close to 14 million role records, and it grows every time somebody looks a channel up. That costs a fixed amount every month whether or not anyone donates.

There is no paid tier, nothing is behind a login, and the api is open. Donations are the only thing keeping it that way.
