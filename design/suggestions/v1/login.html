Settings need a twitch sign-in.

moddex has to know which twitch account is yours before it can let you change anything about it.

You land back on the page you were trying to reach. Nothing on the rest of the site needs an account.

What a signed-in account can do

  • Opt out of the index

    Your profile stops being served and you come off every list and the api.

  • Pick a chat badge

    If you hold more than one, choose which shows next to your name.

  • Attach a donation to your account

    So the donator badge lands automatically instead of by email.

What twitch hands over

Your user id, login name and avatar. moddex never sees your password, cannot read your chat, and cannot act on your behalf. Read the full terms in section 4.
