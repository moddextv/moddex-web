Brackets and corners

Two corners imply a frame without drawing one. A full border would be a card, and a card is the generic shape this design avoids. A single corner marks state, oriented by role: the four orientations are the four corners of the mark, and misc/roles.ts already assigns one to each role.

Badges

Awarded, not decorative. 14px in a table row, 20px on a profile, 44px where the badge itself is the subject. Every one carries its name.

Motion

Four durations, two curves, transform and opacity only. Motion here confirms an action or assembles the mark. It never decorates, and it all collapses to a fade under prefers-reduced-motion.

--duration-press 140ms, scale(0.97) on :active
--duration-hover 180ms, colour and background
--duration-bracket 260ms, corners converging, +60ms on the second
--duration-enter 520ms, staggered 70ms, capped at 480ms
--ease-spring cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)
