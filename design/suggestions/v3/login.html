Settings need a twitch sign-in

moddex has to know which twitch account is yours before it can let you change anything about it. Nothing else on the site needs an account.

You land back on the page you were trying to reach.

What a signed-in account can do

  • Opt out of the index Your profile stops being served and you come off every list and the public api.
  • Pick a chat badge If you hold more than one, choose which shows next to your name.
  • Attach a donation So the donator badge lands automatically instead of by email.
