Motion

Paper does not animate. The restraint is the register.

entry A 420ms fade. No translate, no stagger beyond a beat.
hover on a row The paper tints. No fill, no border, no lift.

loading A sweep across the blank, like light across paper.
reduced motion All of it stops. Nothing is lost, because nothing was carrying meaning.
