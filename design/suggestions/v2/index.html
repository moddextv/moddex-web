Suggestion v2

moddex as an almanac

Nothing here is a new colour. The mark is untouched. v1, the direction you liked

moddex is a directory. Not a dashboard, not a dev tool. Two and a half million entries, cross-referenced two ways, each with a date attached.

The best-designed dense-data objects ever made are printed directories: almanacs, ships' registers, timetables, library catalogues. Centuries of work went into making thousands of rows of names and dates readable on paper, and almost none of it looks like a website.

So this direction takes that typography seriously instead of reaching for another dark canvas with two saturated accents, which is the look every generated interface arrives at.

Where it departs

  Shipped and v1 v2
Ground near-black canvas paper, on a desk
Type Cairo and Lato, a heavy grotesk display Newsreader, set light at display size
Labels tracked-out uppercase, monospace italic serif, sentence case
Tables a hairline under every row rules above, below and at the foot only
Apparatus chips and pills in the content notes in the margin
Numerals tabular everywhere old-style in prose, lining in columns
Motion staggered entrances one fade. paper does not animate

Why the mark fits this

Look at the top-left and bottom-right corners of this sheet.

The moddex mark is two corner brackets in a hundred and eighty degrees of rotational symmetry. That is precisely what a printer's registration mark is, and where it goes is the corner of the sheet. Put the two halves there and the page becomes the logo, at page scale, without drawing it anywhere. Nothing was bent to make this work; it was already true.

Worth arguing about

All of it is reversible.

Two new fonts Newsreader and Archivo come from the Google CDN in these comps. Shipping means self-hosting two woff2 files, which the repo already does for Lato and Cairo, so the pattern exists.
A light interface Every value comes from the light theme already in tailwind.config.mjs, but layout.tsx hardcodes dark on the html element, so shipping this means removing that one class and wiring the theme properly.
A serif interface Unusual for a twitch tool, which is the point, but it is a bigger swing than v1 and it will not suit everyone.
Copy without em-dashes The taste skill bans the character outright, so sentences were restructured. That is a voice change as well as a visual one.
The register Everything on /dashboard below the heading is a proposal. The shipped page is a stub with nothing to redesign.
