How the index fills

Nobody has to sign in, and nothing is queued. The read is the write.

A channel enters the index the first time somebody searches for it. That read pulls its mod and vip lists in and keeps them.

So if a moderator you expect is missing from somebody's entry, the fix is to look up the channel they moderate, once. It stays indexed afterwards, for everyone.
